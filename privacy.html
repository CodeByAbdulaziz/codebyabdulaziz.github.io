WEBSITE INFO

Privacy

I use Google Analytics to understand visits and improve this website. Analytics starts only after you choose Accept analytics.

What analytics can show

Reports can show page views, visit sources, general location, device type, browser, and actions such as scrolling or clicking a link. Location is an estimate. It does not show your exact address.

Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits. I do not send names, email addresses, or passwords to Analytics. This website has no sign-up form. I do not use Google Signals or personalized ads.

Your choice

Choose No thanks to keep analytics off. You can change your choice at any time using Analytics choices in the footer. Turning analytics off stops future collection from this browser. It does not remove data already collected.

Your choice is saved in your browser. If you clear browser data or use another browser or device, you will be asked again. If browser storage is blocked, the choice applies only to the current page.

Google and hosting

Google processes the Analytics data. Read how Google uses information from websites. Google also offers an Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep its own service logs. Read the GitHub privacy statement.
